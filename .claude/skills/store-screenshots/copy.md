# Store screenshot copy

The headline does the selling; the capture proves it. Write every headline before
any layout, and read it at thumbnail size.

## Rules for one headline
- **One idea.** If the headline needs "and", it's two slides.
- **Short, common words.** The reader is scrolling a store, not reading a page.
- **Two lines at most, about 3–5 words a line.** Break lines by hand where the meaning breaks, and keep the lines roughly even.
- **Concrete over abstract.** Name this app's nouns and verbs: what the user does, sees or stops doing. *Clean, seamless, powerful, all-in-one, next-level* say nothing.
- **No claim you can't back.** Numbers, ratings, rankings, "best", "#1", "loved by", press and awards come from the user or don't appear. Mark the gap `[NEEDS PROOF]`.
- **No store chrome.** No price, "Download now", "Free" or store badges.

## Three ways in
Offer three options per slide, one of each:
1. **A moment:** put the reader in the scene where the app helps. *Dinner's in the oven before the timer would have rung.*
2. **An outcome:** say what changes for them. *Every bill split before you leave the table.*
3. **A removed pain:** name the chore that goes away. *No more retyping receipts.*

These examples show the shape; write your own from the app's real features.

## Jobs across the deck
| Slot | Job | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Hero (1) | The one benefit, in one second | Would someone who sees only this slide know what the app is for? |
| Differentiator (2) | Why this app and not the obvious alternative | Could a competitor put this headline on their slide? If yes, rewrite. |
| Features (3–n) | One feature per slide, each with its own capture | Does the capture show exactly what the headline says? |
| Trust (optional) | Real proof: privacy, platform features, real ratings or press | Did the user give you this? If not, cut the slide. |
| Closer | What's left: a short list of smaller features, or one quiet line | Does it add something the earlier slides didn't? |

The first two or three slides carry the deck; most people never swipe further.

## Localizing
Rewrite each headline in the target language rather than translating it word for
word, then check the line breaks again: lengths change. Right-to-left languages
need the layout mirrored where it matters and the text direction set, not just
flipped glyphs. A locale nobody on the team can read goes under Open in the hand-over
until someone who reads it has checked it.

## At thumbnail size
Shrink the slide to about 160px wide. If the headline doesn't read, cut words or
make it bigger; don't add a subtitle to explain it.
